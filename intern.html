Overview This Summer The Full Role
Why This Role Exists

With an agency partner handling the specialist marketing stack — SEO, paid media, website, content, social, reporting — the internal need shifts from strategy to coordination. The value of an internal person becomes execution on the ground and keeping the machine fed.

This person owns three things the agency cannot own from the outside:

  • On-the-ground event execution — booth setup, golf course demos, trade shows, community sponsorships, showroom events
  • Internal information flow — getting inventory updates, promotions, seasonal priorities, photos, and approvals from sales and service to the agency on time
  • Brand presence at the local level — the day-to-day social posts, Google review responses, and community engagement that require someone in the building
Dual-Brand Scope — LEGC + Versatile

With the Versatile acquisition closing in the coming months (~5 locations in Minnesota), the full-time coordinator role is scoped from day one to sit between both brands. The intern position is LEGC Ohio only — a focused starting point. The dual-brand coordination layer gets added when the role goes full-time.

Start With an Intern This Summer

Before committing to a full-time coordinator hire, there's a natural half-step: bring on a marketing intern for the summer. A college student home for break — someone studying marketing, communications, or business — can take on the highest-volume, lowest-risk parts of this role immediately and give LEGC a working prototype of what the coordinator position looks like in practice.

What the Intern Owns

  • Social media content capture and posting — daily photos/video from the showroom, deliveries, and service shop. Post to Facebook and Instagram with brand-consistent captions.
  • Google review responses — reply to reviews within 24 hours using approved templates and tone guidelines
  • Event support — assist with booth setup, on-site presence, lead capture, photo documentation, and post-event follow-up at golf course demos and community events
  • Asset organization — catalog the existing photo/video library, identify gaps, and build an organized asset folder the agency can pull from
  • Agency communication support — help relay basic information (inventory updates, product photos, spec sheets) to the agency on a defined schedule

What the Intern Does Not Own

  • Independent event planning or vendor negotiations
  • Budget decisions of any size — all spend goes through VP Ops
  • Agency strategy calls or creative direction
  • Brand messaging decisions or sponsorship commitments
  • Versatile coordination — intern scope is LEGC Ohio only

Intern Compensation

$15–$18/hour, 30–40 hours/week, 10–12 weeks (June–August). Total cost: approximately $5,400–$8,640 for the summer. No benefits required. This is a fraction of the coordinator salary and gives LEGC real data on what kind of person they need for the full-time role — plus a summer's worth of content, organized assets, and event presence they wouldn't otherwise have.

What LEGC Learns

The intern isn't just cheap labor — they're a test run. By the end of summer, LEGC will know:

  • How much time social media and review responses actually take per week
  • What the agency actually needs from an internal contact (and what falls through the cracks without one)
  • Whether the event workload justifies a dedicated person or can be absorbed into existing roles
  • What skills to prioritize in the full-time coordinator hire — informed by real experience, not guesswork

Best case: the intern is strong enough to convert into the full-time coordinator role after graduation. You've already trained them, they know the business, and the agency relationship is already established. Either way: LEGC enters the coordinator hiring process in the fall with a much clearer picture of what the role actually requires day-to-day.

Marketing Intern — Lake Erie Golf Cars

Location: Warrensville Heights, OH (with travel to Canfield location and event venues throughout NEO)
Type: Seasonal / Part-time (30–40 hours per week)
Duration: June–August 2026 (10–12 weeks)
Reports to: VP Operations / General Manager
Compensation: $15–$18/hour

About Lake Erie Golf Cars

Lake Erie Golf Cars has been Northeast Ohio's trusted source for golf carts for 40 years. As an authorized E-Z-GO dealer serving 100+ golf courses and thousands of residential, commercial, and recreational customers across two locations, we're growing — and we're looking for a summer marketing intern to help us build our brand presence, capture content from the field, and support our busiest event season.

The Role

You'll be embedded with our team for the summer — capturing social media content from the showroom and events, responding to customer reviews, helping coordinate event logistics, and organizing our marketing asset library. You'll work alongside our external marketing agency and get hands-on experience with real-world marketing operations at a growing company. This is a doing role, not a watching role.

What You'll Do

  • Capture and post social media content (photos, short video) from the showroom, deliveries, service shop, and events
  • Respond to Google reviews and social media comments using brand guidelines
  • Assist with event logistics: booth setup, on-site presence, lead capture, and photo documentation at golf course demos and community events
  • Organize and catalog the existing photo and video library into a structured asset folder
  • Help relay information to our marketing agency — inventory updates, product photos, spec sheets
  • Track promotional materials inventory (brochures, signage, event supplies)
  • Support post-event follow-up: lead handoff documentation and thank-you communications

What We're Looking For

  • Currently pursuing a degree in marketing, communications, business, or a related field
  • Active on social media with a sense for what makes good visual content
  • Organized and reliable — you show up on time and follow through
  • Comfortable taking photos and short videos with a phone
  • Available 30–40 hours per week, June through August
  • Able to work occasional evenings or weekends for events during the summer
  • Willing to travel between our two Ohio locations (mileage reimbursed)

Nice to Have

  • Interest in golf carts, powersports, or outdoor recreation
  • Basic photo or video editing skills (phone apps are fine)
  • Event, retail, or customer service experience
  • Own transportation (mileage between locations is reimbursed at $0.70/mile)

What We Offer

  • $15–$18/hour, 30–40 hours per week
  • Real-world marketing experience at a 40-year-old company with a loyal customer base
  • Hands-on event coordination during LEGC's busiest season
  • A portfolio of social media content and event documentation you can take with you
  • Exposure to professional agency workflow — how marketing actually runs at a multi-location business
  • Mileage reimbursement for travel between locations and to events
  • Potential path to a full-time marketing coordinator position after graduation
