Executive Summary

The Situation

As LEGC explores a new marketing structure — with specialist work like SEO, paid media, website, content, and analytics handled by an agency partner — the internal need shifts from strategy to coordination. With the Versatile acquisition closing in the coming months (~5 Minnesota locations), that coordination now spans two brands across two states. The gap: who handles on-the-ground event execution, internal information flow to the agency, and local brand presence that can only come from someone in the building?

The Recommendation

If this direction makes sense, the lowest-risk starting point is a summer intern (June–August 2026) to prototype the role at minimal cost. What LEGC learns over the summer informs the full-time marketing coordinator hire — a role that would sit between both brands, with the salary split across both P&Ls.
