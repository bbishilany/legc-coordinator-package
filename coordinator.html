04 Decision Authority & Budget Tiers

The coordinator needs defined authority to keep things moving without bottlenecking on approvals for routine decisions.

Tier Authority Examples
Green — Autonomous Coordinator decides, no approval needed Social media posts, review responses, routine event logistics, photo/video capture, asset sharing with agency, routine content approvals
Amber — Inform Coordinator decides, notifies VP Ops same day Mid-range event spend, vendor selections for existing events, promotional material reorders, scheduling changes
Red — Approve Requires VP Ops sign-off before proceeding Significant event spend, new event commitments, sponsorship agreements, brand messaging changes, agency scope discussions, any spend outside the approved annual budget

Annual Event Budget

Recommend establishing a fixed annual event budget that the coordinator manages within the Green and Amber tiers above. This gives them the room to execute without death-by-approval on every booth fee, while keeping significant commitments in leadership's hands.

05 Qualifications

Required

  • 1–3 years in marketing coordination, event coordination, or a similar operational marketing role
  • Event planning experience — trade shows, demos, community events, or similar
  • Strong organizational skills — managing calendars, deadlines, vendor relationships, and multiple projects simultaneously
  • Comfortable working with external agencies — routing feedback, managing approvals, keeping timelines on track
  • Social media fluency — ability to post real-time content, respond to community engagement, and maintain a consistent brand voice

Occasional travel to Versatile's Minnesota locations as needed. Mileage reimbursement provided (see compensation).
  • Willingness to work occasional evenings and weekends for events, particularly April through October

Preferred

  • Experience in automotive, powersports, dealership, or recreational vehicle industries
  • Familiarity with basic marketing analytics (reading a Google Analytics report, understanding ad performance numbers the agency sends over)
  • Photography/video capture skills (iPhone-level is fine — not production)
  • Experience managing marketing budgets or event budgets
  • Bachelor's degree in marketing, communications, or business (practical experience weighted equally)
  • Multi-location or multi-brand experience

Not Required

The following are handled by the agency and should not be part of the hiring criteria:

  • SEO, SEM, or paid media management
  • Website development or CMS management
  • Graphic design or ad creative production
  • Marketing strategy development
  • Analytics or reporting infrastructure

09 Growth Path

Coordinator-level roles have 18–24 month turnover risk if there is no visible path forward. Defining that path upfront signals long-term investment and attracts better candidates.

  • Summer 2026 (Intern): Learn the business, capture content, support events, build the asset library. Low cost, high learning — for both the intern and LEGC.
  • Year 1 (Coordinator): Master the agency workflow, build event playbook, establish brand presence cadence. Own the execution. If the intern converts, they already have a head start.
  • Year 2 (Senior Coordinator): Take ownership of event budget and ROI reporting. Begin managing vendor relationships independently. Potential title bump to Senior Marketing Coordinator ($55–60K) if KPIs met.
  • Year 3+ (Marketing Manager): As LEGC + Versatile grow (additional locations, new acquisitions), this role could evolve into a Marketing Manager overseeing junior coordinators per brand — while the agency relationship continues to handle the specialist work.

This path is not guaranteed — it depends on performance and business growth. But starting with an intern this summer and defining the full trajectory from day one attracts people who want to build something, not just fill a seat.

About Lake Erie Golf Cars

Lake Erie Golf Cars has been Northeast Ohio's trusted source for golf carts for 40 years. As an authorized E-Z-GO dealer serving 100+ golf courses and thousands of residential, commercial, and recreational customers across two locations, we're growing — and we're looking for a marketing coordinator to be the connective tissue between our team, our agency partner, and our community.

The Role

You'll be the internal marketing point person — coordinating events, managing the flow of information between our team and our external marketing agency, keeping our social media active with real content from the showroom and the field, and making sure both of our locations are represented in everything we do. You'll also coordinate remotely with our sister brand as we grow. This is a hands-on coordination role, not a strategy role. Our agency handles SEO, advertising, website, and analytics. You keep the engine running on the ground.

What You'll Do

  • Plan and execute events: golf course demos, trade shows, community sponsorships, showroom events, and grand openings
  • Serve as the primary contact between our team and our marketing agency — relay priorities, route approvals, share assets, and keep campaigns on schedule
  • Post real-time social media content from events, deliveries, the showroom floor, and the service shop
  • Respond to Google reviews and social media comments
  • Coordinate across our Warrensville Heights and Canfield locations on marketing needs
  • Capture photos and video from daily operations for use in marketing materials
  • Manage event budgets, vendor relationships, and promotional materials inventory
  • Track leads from events and ensure follow-up happens
  • Maintain and organize our marketing asset library (photos, logos, spec sheets, signage)

What We're Looking For

  • 1–3 years in marketing coordination, event coordination, or a similar role
  • Hands-on event planning experience — you've run a booth, managed vendors, and handled the logistics
  • Organized and reliable — you manage calendars, hit deadlines, and keep multiple projects moving
  • Comfortable with social media — you can post in the moment and keep a brand voice consistent
  • Good communicator — you'll sit between our leadership team and an external agency, and both need to trust you
  • Willing to travel between our two Ohio locations and to event venues across Northeast Ohio (mileage reimbursed)
  • Available for occasional evenings and weekends during event season (April–October)

Nice to Have

  • Experience in automotive, powersports, or recreational vehicle industries
  • Multi-location or multi-brand experience
  • Comfortable reading basic marketing reports (the agency provides them — you just need to understand what they mean)
  • Photography or video skills (phone-level is fine)

What We Offer

  • Competitive salary ($48,000–$52,000)
  • Health insurance, paid time off, and company benefits
  • Mileage reimbursement for travel between locations and to events
  • A 40-year-old company with a strong reputation and loyal customer base
  • A role where you're the go-to person, not buried in a department
  • A professional agency partnership that handles the technical marketing — you focus on what happens on the ground
  • Growth opportunity as we expand to additional brands and locations
